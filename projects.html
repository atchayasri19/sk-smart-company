About the Concept

A Warm Space for Family Meals

Spice Garden is a demo restaurant. The site opens with the dining room so guests know what to expect, then moves into the food. Each recipe has its own card with a photo and a short note.

The menu covers South Indian breakfast, rice dishes, grills, curries and vegetarian thalis. Everything can be updated without redesigning the page.

The Experience

Relaxed Seating for Every Occasion

The dining room is set up for quiet family dinners, small celebrations and weekend lunches with friends. Tables are spaced so groups can talk without raising their voices.

Warm lighting, wooden tables and a clear view into the kitchen make the room feel welcoming from the moment a guest walks in. Children's seating and a garden area are available on request.
